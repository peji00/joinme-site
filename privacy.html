Join Me — Privacy Policy

Written to satisfy the Swiss revFADP and the EU GDPR. Prepared with software assistance and published under the operator's responsibility.

Controller: Zahra Sohrabi, Lausanne, Switzerland — saba.sohrabii.94@gmail.com. No data protection officer is appointed (not required at our size); the controller handles privacy requests directly.

1. The short version

We collect what the app visibly needs and nothing else: your account email, the profile you write, the plans you post, your messages, and technical data needed to run the service. No advertising, no trackers, no sale of data, no profiling. Your data lives in the EU (Frankfurt). Exact meeting places and group chats are visible only to the people in the plan. You can see, correct, and delete everything yourself in the app.

Location, in plain words: if you tap "Use my location" when choosing your city, the app asks the operating system for a coarse position once, the operating system's geocoding service turns it into a city name, and the position is then discarded. That position never reaches us or anyone working for us; only the city name you end up selecting — the same string you could have typed — scopes which plans you see. The selected city is remembered on your device only.

3. Who can see what (privacy by design)

  • Signed-out visitors: plan titles, areas, times, and hosts' name/photo of active plans only. No profiles, no exact venues, no member lists.
  • Signed-in members: profiles of hosts and fellow group members; approximate areas of all plans.
  • A plan's host: the profiles and messages of people who ask to join.
  • A plan's group (host + accepted members): the exact venue, the group chat, read receipts, participant list.
  • Nobody else. These boundaries are enforced in the database itself (row-level security), not just in the app's interface.
  • Activity counts and history are visible only to you.

5. Retention

  • Account data: for the life of your account.
  • Account deletion (in-app: Profile → Delete account) permanently deletes your profile, photos, plans, join requests, messages, read receipts, blocks/reports you filed, subscription mirror, and push tokens — immediately and irreversibly at the database level.
  • Backups of the database roll off automatically within up to 30 days.
  • Subscription records held by Apple/Google/RevenueCat follow their retention rules (we cannot delete Apple's own billing history).
  • Reports about you filed by others may be retained in anonymized/necessary form where required for safety or legal defense.

6. Your rights

Under the revFADP and (where it applies) the GDPR you can: access your data, correct it (in-app: Edit profile), delete it (in-app: Delete account), receive a copy (portability), object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent (e.g. disable notifications in device settings). Write to saba.sohrabii.94@gmail.com — we answer within 30 days.

You may complain to the FDPIC (Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, Bern) or, for GDPR matters, to your local EU supervisory authority.

7. Security

Row-level security on every table, TLS everywhere, least-privilege access rules verified by automated security tests, no third-party analytics or ad SDKs. No system is perfectly secure; in case of a breach likely to result in high risk to you, we will notify the FDPIC (and affected users where required) in line with legal deadlines.

8. Children

Join Me is for adults — 18+. We do not knowingly process children's data; accounts found to belong to minors are deleted.

9. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app before they take effect.
